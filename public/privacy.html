MoodMap AI

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 1, 2025

Your privacy is fundamental to MoodMap AI. This policy explains what data we collect, how it is stored, and your rights as a user. We have designed MoodMap AI with a privacy-first architecture: your personal data stays on your device.

MoodMap AI stores all your mood data locally on your device. We do not upload, sell, or share your personal check-in data with any third party.

1. What Data We Collect

When you use MoodMap AI, the following information may be recorded and stored locally on your device:

  • Mood scores — numerical ratings (1–10) you enter during check-ins
  • Activities and context — tags you select (e.g. work, exercise, social) and free-text notes
  • Location data — optional GPS coordinates associated with a check-in (see Section 3)
  • Voice notes — optional audio recordings you make during a check-in (see Section 4)
  • Physical sensations — body-awareness tags you choose to log
  • Device information — basic device identifiers used solely for subscription management via RevenueCat (see Section 5)

We do not collect your name, email address, or any account credentials unless you explicitly create an account.

2. How Data Is Stored

All mood check-in data — including scores, activities, notes, voice recordings, and location coordinates — is stored exclusively in a local SQLite database on your device using expo-sqlite. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to our servers or any external service.

Backups of this data may occur through your device's standard OS backup mechanisms (iCloud on iOS, Google Drive on Android) if you have those services enabled. These backups are governed by Apple's and Google's respective privacy policies.

We do not operate any servers that receive, process, or store your personal mood data.

3. Location Data

Location access is entirely optional. MoodMap AI will only request location permission if you choose to enable the "Emotional GPS" feature. You can use the app fully without ever granting location access.

When location is enabled:

  • Coordinates are stored locally on your device alongside the relevant check-in
  • You can enable privacy blur, which reduces coordinate precision so your exact address is never recorded
  • Location data is never transmitted to our servers or shared with third parties
  • You can delete all location data at any time via the Data Export & Delete screen

4. Voice Notes

Voice notes are an optional feature. If you choose to record a voice note during a check-in, the audio file is stored locally on your device only. Voice recordings are never transmitted to any server, never processed by a remote speech-recognition service, and never shared with third parties.

On-device transcription (if available) uses Apple's or Google's built-in speech frameworks, which are governed by their respective privacy policies.

5. Third-Party Services

MoodMap AI uses a limited number of third-party services:

  • RevenueCat — used to manage in-app subscriptions and purchases. RevenueCat may collect a device identifier and purchase history to validate your subscription status. This data is used solely for subscription management and is governed by RevenueCat's Privacy Policy. We do not sell this data.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — payment processing for subscriptions is handled entirely by Apple or Google. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

We do not use advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or social media SDKs.

6. Your Rights

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • Access — all your data is viewable within the app
  • Export — use the "Export Data" feature to download a copy of all your check-ins in a portable format
  • Delete — use the "Delete All Data" option in Settings to permanently erase all locally stored data from your device

Because your data is stored only on your device, exercising these rights does not require contacting us — you can do it directly within the app at any time.

7. GDPR Compliance

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing of, and port your personal data.

Because MoodMap AI processes all personal data locally on your device and does not transmit it to our servers, we act as a data processor only for the limited device identifier data handled by RevenueCat for subscription purposes. For any GDPR-related requests, please contact us at scharmch@gmail.com.

8. CCPA Compliance

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to delete personal information, and the right to opt out of the sale of personal information.

We do not sell your personal information. All mood and check-in data remains on your device. For any CCPA-related requests, contact us at scharmch@gmail.com.

9. Children's Privacy

MoodMap AI is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the app, please contact us at scharmch@gmail.com and we will take appropriate steps to address it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Effective date" at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of MoodMap AI after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
