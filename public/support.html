Frequently Asked Questions

How do I export my data?

Open the app and go to Settings → Export Data. You can export all your check-ins as a CSV or JSON file, which you can save to your device or share to another app. Your data belongs to you and is always available for export.

How do I delete all my data?

Go to Settings → Delete All Data. This permanently erases all check-ins, mood scores, voice notes, and location data stored on your device. This action cannot be undone. Because all data is stored locally, deletion is immediate and complete — nothing is retained on any server.

How do I set or change my check-in reminders?

Go to Settings → Reminders. You can set one or more daily reminder times, choose which days of the week to receive them, and toggle reminders on or off at any time. Make sure notifications are enabled for MoodMap AI in your device's system settings.

What are AI Insights and how do they work?

AI Insights analyze the patterns in your local check-in history to surface observations about your emotional triggers, safe spaces, and stress patterns. The analysis runs on your device using your stored data — it is not diagnostic, not medical advice, and is intended purely for self-reflection. Insights improve as you log more check-ins over time.

What does MoodMap AI Pro include?

MoodMap AI Pro unlocks unlimited AI Insights, advanced mood analytics, weekly AI-generated reports, and priority access to new features. Free users can still log check-ins and view their mood history with no limits.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed entirely through the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android). To cancel or change your plan:

iOS: Open the Settings app → tap your Apple ID → Subscriptions → MoodMap AI → Cancel Subscription.

Android: Open the Google Play Store → tap your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → MoodMap AI → Cancel.

We are unable to process refunds directly — please contact Apple or Google support for billing disputes.

I purchased Pro but it's not showing as active. What do I do?

First, try pulling down to refresh on the paywall or settings screen to restore your purchase. If that doesn't work, tap Restore Purchases on the paywall screen. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account used for the original purchase. If the issue persists, email us at scharmch@gmail.com with your order number and we'll help sort it out.

Is my data private? Does MoodMap AI share it?

Yes, your data is completely private. All mood check-ins, voice notes, and location data are stored locally on your device only and are never uploaded to our servers or shared with third parties. See our full Privacy Policy for details.

The app is crashing or behaving unexpectedly. What should I do?

Try force-quitting and reopening the app. If the issue persists, try uninstalling and reinstalling (your data is stored locally, so reinstalling will erase it — export your data first if you want to keep it). If you're still experiencing problems, email us at scharmch@gmail.com with a description of what happened and your device model and OS version.
